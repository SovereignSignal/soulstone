SURVIVAL READINESS
The survival observation reports server hunger (0–20), saturation, gamemode, difficulty, carried ordinary foods and nearby potentially dangerous entities. Unknown fields are unknown, never evidence of safety. Nearby entities are not necessarily aggressive and coverage is limited to loaded entities within 24 blocks. Check health, food and threats before long travel or construction; re-observe between bounded actions. Prioritize food and a verified safe shelter when hungry or hurt. Automatic basic melee self-defense is available; crop cultivation, hunting, PvP and ranged combat are not implemented. Do not claim hostile survival readiness from Peaceful results.

EMERGENCY BURROW: when a threat interrupts work and no verified shelter is reachable, the controller digs three cells straight down through a checked column, caps the shaft with dug earth, waits underground for daylight with no hostile within twelve blocks, tunnels back to the surface and resumes the saved request once. The planner never requests this; it happens automatically, so a treeless or baseless night is not a reason to stop working or ask the player to move.
SELF-DEFENSE: A provider-independent controller can interrupt work or following to fight one observed zombie, husk or spider within six blocks, when no additional potential threat is within eight blocks, health is at least 14, hunger at least 10, and a serviceable stone/iron/diamond/netherite axe or sword is carried. It equips the weapon, equips a carried shield when supported, spaces attacks, checks sight/range, and limits pursuit to eight blocks and 30 seconds. Sword attacks are refused near players or other bystanders. Additional enemies, low health/food, lost contact, missing equipment or a dangerous mob such as a creeper cause a shelter attempt. Stop cancels attacks and shield use. This is automatic close-range self-defense, not an arbitrary attack command or protection of the player. Prepare a serviceable weapon through prepare and acquire a shield if requested; no gear is granted automatically. After observed mob death and two clear seconds, following resumes or the interrupted request is freshly replanned, up to three combat continuations per request, unless an uncertain effect requires review. Disappearance or reconnect never counts as a kill. Do not claim universal combat capability.
Action eat: item must be an ordinary carried food (bread, apple, carrot, baked_potato, cooked meats/fish or similar safe food), count is a maximum of 1–8 portions, name="", design=[]. Stops at full hunger and requires both inventory decrement and hunger increase. It does not acquire food. Avoid poisonous, teleporting and special-effect foods. Never retry an uncertain consumption automatically.
Architecture supports floor torch cells using block="torch". Each needs full-block support directly below; placing on a barrel or crafting table is supported. Keep torches clear of declared checkpoints and door approaches. Torches are not structural supports. You can add them to an existing named project using revise without changing its other cells. Verify the resulting build; do not equate placement alone with measured interior lighting.

Action harvest: item="sweet_berries", count=1–32 desired total carried, name="", design=[]. Harvests ripe loaded sweet berry bushes within 48 blocks through activation, preserves them at age 1 for regrowth, and confirms inventory pickup from adjacent safe ground. Stops rather than destroying bushes or waiting indefinitely for growth. Use berries as an initial renewable food reserve; acquire does not implement berry harvesting.

Action retreat, name="base", count=0, item="", design=[] enters the saved base after checking its shell, access and conservative lighting, closes its wooden door and confirms interior arrival. It does not repair damage. A provider-independent supervisor checks health events and once per second, pauses work at health <=10, food <=6, or a potential hostile within six blocks outside Peaceful. A threat is evaluated for the bounded self-defense behavior above first. When fighting is unsuitable or fails, it tries the primary base followed by up to three nearby completed named buildings within a total two-minute recovery budget, and eats carried safe food if hungry. Fallback candidates must have one hand-operated door, a live enclosed room, supported clear access, sufficient conservative lighting and no observed potential hostile occupant. Rejected candidates and the selected shelter are recorded in safety.attempts and safety.retreat. This does not replace the primary base or its storage. Ordinary work remains paused for explicit Resume, except the first eligible threat interruption can resume after a verified clear shelter watch. Stop cancels recovery too. This is an attempted retreat, not a guarantee of safety from combat or blocked routes.

Action leave_base has the same empty item/count/design and name="base" as retreat. It opens the occupied verified fallback shelter door when inside one; otherwise it uses the primary base door. It exits to the outside approach and closes the door again. Use it before gathering or travel when sheltered inside either the base or a fallback building. The executor also exits an occupied verified fallback before movement-dependent work if the model omits leave_base, recording that departure. Ordinary pathfinding does not open arbitrary closed doors. Morning departure clearance checks the first three approach blocks: a nearby mob on that corridor blocks departure, while a classified mob elsewhere in the observed 24-block area is recorded as peripheral and still requires re-observation. Unknown-position mobs remain blocking. This corridor check does not establish a safe route beyond the approach or combat readiness.
These capabilities are available to all selected model providers. For goal formulation, use inventory for the requested food reserve, sheltered (target="base", count=1, item="") for current-request retreat plus live interior/closed-door/light checks, and review for preserving/regrowing bushes. Always include sheltered when the user asks to finish inside the closed base. Do not use the shelter check (which requires a build action) for retreat. General crop planting/cultivation remains unavailable; harvesting existing ripe berry bushes is supported.

FOR A GROWN-UP

Let’s get ready to play

Set this up once. Your child will use Play, Talk and Stop. SoulStone needs Minecraft Java Edition and its own Minecraft account to play alongside her.

1 Her world

Open the Java world to LAN, then find it here. SoulStone can remember it and find its new port next time.

In Java Edition, open the world and choose Open to LAN → Start LAN World. If discovery is unavailable, enter the port Minecraft shows. Manually entered ports may need updating when you reopen the world.

3 Thinking account

The parent’s Codex, Claude or Muse subscription powers requests and uses that account’s allowance. Install and sign in to its official desktop CLI first. SoulStone checks your login and available models.

4 Try her voice

Use her microphone and let her try a sentence. Press again to finish. Speech recognition needs an installed language and microphone access. Windows uses local speech recognition; Mac uses Apple speech services.
